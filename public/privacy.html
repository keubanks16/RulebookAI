Privacy Policy

Last updated November 13, 2026

The Rulebook AI gives coaches and parents an AI reading of sports rules. This page explains what the app stores and why.

What we collect

  • Your account: your email address and a scrambled (hashed) copy of your password.
  • Your rulings: the plays you describe, the options you pick (sport, rule book, age group) and the answers, so you can see them again.
  • Subscription status: if you subscribe, Stripe handles the payment. We receive the subscription status and renewal date, never your card number.

How we use it

Only to run the app: answering your questions, saving your rulings, counting free rulings and turning on your subscription. We do not sell personal information and we do not show ads.

Services we use

  • Anthropic answers the rules questions. It receives the play you describe and the options you pick, not your email.
  • Cloudflare runs the app and stores your rulings.
  • Stripe processes subscriptions.

Keep names out of it

Describe plays without players’ full names or other personal details. The app does not need them.

Children

Accounts are for adults: coaches, parents and guardians.

Deleting your information

In the app, open Account and tap Delete account. That removes your sign-in and your saved rulings. Cancel a subscription first.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above changes with it.
